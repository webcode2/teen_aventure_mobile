TBA APP TESTING AND FEEDBACK REPORT
1. Overview
The TBA App was reviewed using the available demo access points for the different user categories: Teen, Underaged Teen, Parent, and Teacher/Facilitator.
The review focused on the overall interface, navigation, content presentation, user experience, and the functions available to each user category.
Overall, the app interface appears well structured and functional across the different access points tested. The opening pages and general user interfaces are clear and easy to navigate. Only a few areas require clarification or further consideration to ensure that the final app aligns fully with the existing TBA curriculum and resources.
