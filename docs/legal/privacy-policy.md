# Privacy Policy — Teen Bible Adventures

**Last updated:** 29 August 2026  
**Audience:** ages 13 and older. This app is not directed at children under 13.

Host this page at a public HTTPS URL and paste that URL into Google Play Console → App content → Privacy policy. The same text is shown in-app under Profile → Privacy Policy.

## Data on the device

Version 1 of the Play Store build does not create an online account. Scores, streak, and settings stay on the device (local storage).

## Network

If the publisher configures a production API, the app may fetch lesson lists over HTTPS. Do not put personal data in lesson titles sent to that API.

## YouTube

Lessons may play through YouTube’s in-app player. Google’s privacy policy applies to that playback. This app does not download YouTube videos.

## Ads and sale of data

No ads. We do not sell personal information.

## Contact

Use the developer email listed on the Google Play store listing.
