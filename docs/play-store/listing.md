# Play Store listing copy

**Title (max 30):** Teen Bible Adventures

**Short description (max 80):** Bible quests, YouTube lessons, and quizzes for teens exploring God's Word.

**Full description:**

Teen Bible Adventures is a discipleship app for teens 13 and older.

Explore a weekly Bible-in-a-Year quest, watch lessons on YouTube in the app, and take quizzes that help you remember what you read. Earn gems and keep a streak as you stay in the Word.

This app is free and has no ads. It is not designed for children under 13.

YouTube videos stream in the official player. We do not download YouTube videos.
