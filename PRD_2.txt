1. Product Overview & Architectural Context

ASK Hub is an ecosystem for digital discipleship serving children, teens, families, 
and churches. Under the modular architecture, the system is separated into two 
interconnected applications/modules:

- App 1 (ASK Hub Discipleship & Class Engine): Handles Authentication, Family 
  Isolation, Church & Class Management, Teen Quests (Weekly Lessons, Objective 
  Quizzes, Cross Scrambles), Class Leaderboards with Role-Aware Privacy Blurring, 
  Together Mode, Predefined Nudges, Safeguarding Reports, and Admin Management.
  
- App 2 (ASK Hub Spiritual Growth & Content Hub - PRD 2): Dedicated companion app 
  and standalone module providing personal and family spiritual growth experiences:
  1. Scripture Marinade
  2. Prayers of a Lifetime
  3. Devotionals & Daily Studies
  4. Bible in a Year (BIY) Quest & Quizzes
  5. Life & Living Prayers
  6. Bible Reading Tracker

Both applications share the central user identity system (Parents, Guardians, Children, 
Teens, Teachers, Leads, Pastors, App Managers), unified scoring ledger, streaks, and 
family jars while providing focused mobile app views and administrative dashboard tools.

2. Shared User Roles & Access Architecture

All user roles remain consistent across both App 1 and App 2:

Role                   Primary Responsibility in App 2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Parent / Guardian      Manage family profiles, initiate family devotional sessions, 
                       view private family reflections, and track family progress.

Child                  Participate in Scripture Marinade, Prayer steps, BIY, 
                       and Bible Reading under parent-mediated PIN authentication.

Teenager               Access spiritual growth content via independent or parent-
                       managed login; track personal reading and reflections.

Teacher / Chaperone    View aggregate class engagement metrics without violating 
                       family reflection privacy.

Children's Church Lead Access church-level content schedules and devotional metrics.

Pastor / Nominee       High-level oversight of spiritual growth engagement across 
                       church families.

App Manager            Manage global content library (Scripture steps, Prayers, 
                       Devotionals, BIY weeks, Life prayers) via Admin Dashboard.

3. Scoped Features (PRD 2)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Scripture Marinade (`scriptureMarinadeRoutes`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Interactive multi-step Scripture meditation, memorization, and 
  reflection experience.
- Capabilities:
  * Serve sequential marinade steps with scripture text, reflection prompts, 
    and audio guide indicators.
  * Mobile App: Smooth step navigation, audio controls, and memory aids.
  * Scoring: Awards standard activity points upon step completion into the ScoreLedger.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 Prayers of a Lifetime (`prayerRoutes`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Guided prayer journeys for children and teens, including foundational 
  faith milestones.
- Capabilities:
  * Guided prayer steps with structured reflection.
  * Dedicated Salvation Step milestone experience.
  * Mobile App: Interactive prayer cards with step-by-step progress.
  * Scoring: Standard prayer step (28 pts) and Salvation step (10 pts).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 Devotionals & Daily Studies (`devotionalRoutes`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Day-by-day devotional readings tailored to age bands.
- Capabilities:
  * Daily Bible study fetching by day number or list.
  * Private Family Reflection: Parents and children can record private devotional 
    notes and reflections.
  * Privacy Safeguard: Reflections are strictly family-isolated (`request.familyId`) 
    and invisible to teachers, pastors, or external users.
  * Mobile App: Calendar/day selector, rich text reading view, and private journal.
  * Scoring: Completed devotional awards standard points (78 pts).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 Bible in a Year (BIY) Quest (`biyRoutes`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Structured 52-week Bible reading program with video lessons and 
  standalone weekly quizzes.
- Capabilities:
  * Serve BIY weekly reading plans, Scripture references, video embeds/links, 
    and structured JSON quiz payloads.
  * Weekly Scoring Limit: Maximum weekly cap of 78 points per child to prevent 
    gaming or artificial point accumulation.
  * Mobile App: Video player integration, weekly progress tracking, and interactive 
    standalone BIY quiz.
  * Admin Dashboard: Manage 52-week curriculum content, video URLs, and quiz items.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 Life & Living Prayers (`lifePrayerRoutes`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Practical prayer library categorized for daily life scenarios.
- Capabilities:
  * Categorized prayer repository: Morning, Evening, Anytime, Hard Times, Gratitude.
  * Features: Favoriting prayers, custom category filtering, and audio stream links.
  * Mobile App: Quick-access prayer cards, audio player controls, and favorites list.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 Bible Reading Tracker (`bibleReadingRoutes`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Personal Bible reading log tracking Old and New Testament chapter progress.
- Capabilities:
  * Complete chapter logging per child/teen user.
  * Overview API (`/api/hub/bible-reading/books`): Book-by-book total chapters 
    versus completed chapters breakdown (e.g. Genesis 50/50, Matthew 12/28).
  * Mobile App: Visual progress bars per Bible book, chapter grid toggles, and total 
    Bible reading percentage display.

4. Cross-App Integration & Impact

4.1 Unified Score & Ledger Integration
- All spiritual growth activities completed in App 2 generate immutable entries 
  in the shared `ScoreLedger` with appropriate `ActivityType` values 
  (`SCRIPTURE_MARINADE`, `PRAYER_STEP`, `DEVOTIONAL`, `BIY_QUIZ`, `BIBLE_READING`).
- Shared points directly contribute to:
  * The child's unified ASK Score.
  * Daily and weekly streaks.
  * Family Jar contributions and Group Jar aggregates.

4.2 Mobile Application Impact
- Navigation: The mobile app presents a dedicated "Spiritual Growth Hub" tab 
  housing Scripture Marinade, Prayers, Devotionals, BIY, Life Prayers, and 
  Bible Reading Tracker.
- Independent Teen & Child Mode: Seamless switching between Teen Quests (App 1) 
  and Personal Growth Hub (App 2).

4.3 Admin Dashboard Impact
- Content Management System (CMS): Dedicated management interfaces for:
  * Authoring and editing Devotionals.
  * Managing BIY 52-week plans and quizzes.
  * Curating Scripture Marinade steps and Life Prayers.
- Analytics & Engagement Metrics: App Managers can view aggregate completion 
  rates across devotionals, BIY weeks, and Bible reading chapters.

5. Technical Data Model & Route Mapping (App 2)

Prisma Models Involved:
- `Devotional`, `DevotionalReflection`
- `ScriptureStep`, `PrayerStep`
- `BiyWeek`
- `LifePrayer`
- `BibleProgress`
- `ScoreLedger` (shared ledger)

API Endpoints (Prefix: `/api/hub`):
- `GET /api/hub/scripture-marinade/steps`
- `GET /api/hub/prayers/steps`
- `GET /api/hub/devotionals`, `GET /api/hub/devotionals?day=:day`
- `POST /api/hub/devotionals/:id/reflect` (Family private)
- `GET /api/hub/biy/weeks`, `GET /api/hub/biy/weeks/:weekNumber`
- `GET /api/hub/life-prayers?category=:category`
- `POST /api/hub/bible-reading/complete`, `GET /api/hub/bible-reading/progress`
- `GET /api/hub/bible-reading/books`
