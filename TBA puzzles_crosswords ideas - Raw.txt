Mechanic: Solve each clue. The next answer starts from a specific letter of the previous answer, alternating down → across → down → across.
Let’s start with 10 connected answers:
1. MANNA ↓
The food God provided for Israel in the wilderness.
From the 3rd letter of MANNA:
2. NUMBERS →
The book that records much of Israel’s wilderness journey.
From the 4th letter of NUMBERS:
3. BRASS SERPENT ↓
What Moses made and lifted up after the people were bitten by fiery serpents.
From the S in BRASS SERPENT:
4. SINAI →
The mountain where Israel received God’s commandments.
From the 4th letter of SINAI:
5. AARON ↓
Moses’ brother who became Israel’s first high priest.
From the 1st letter of AARON:
6. AMALEK →
The people Israel fought after leaving Egypt.
From the last letter of AMALEK:
7. KADESH ↓
The place from which Moses sent the twelve spies into Canaan.
From the 4th letter of KADESH:
8. EDOM →
The nation descended from Esau that Israel encountered on the journey.
From the 2nd letter of EDOM:
9. DATHAN ↓
The man associated with Korah’s rebellion against Moses.
From the 3rd letter of DATHAN:
10. TABERNACLE →
The movable sanctuary at the centre of Israel’s camp.
This gives us the actual connective skeleton. The important part now is that when we draw it, every specified letter must physically be the same square where the next word begins—no cheating with approximate crossings.

TYPES & SHADOWS
Can you identify the Old Testament picture from its New Testament fulfilment?
How to play: Solve each clue. Then follow the indicated letter to find where the next answer begins.
1. ↓
📖 Exodus 12:1–13 + 1 Corinthians 5:7
A lamb without blemish was chosen. Its blood was placed on the doorposts, and judgment passed over the homes marked by the blood. Centuries later, Paul uses this same picture when describing Christ.
What is the Old Testament shadow?
Answer: PASSOVER
Start Puzzle 2 from the 3rd letter.
2. →
 Numbers 21:4–9 + John 3:14–15
Israel was bitten by fiery serpents because of their rebellion. Moses was instructed to make a particular object and lift it up so that those who looked at it would live. Jesus later directly connected this event to Himself.
What was lifted up?
Answer: SERPENT
 Start Puzzle 3 from the 3rd letter.
3. ↓
 Exodus 25:8–9 + Hebrews 9:1–12
God told Israel to make a place where He would dwell among them. Priests served there, sacrifices were offered, and access to the holiest place was restricted. Hebrews later explains a greater reality associated with this earthly arrangement.
What was this earthly structure called?
Answer: TABERNACLE
 Start Puzzle 4 from the 9th letter.
4. →
📖 Exodus 12:3–6 + John 1:29
Israel's deliverance involved an innocent animal chosen and sacrificed. When John saw Jesus, he used the name of this same animal to identify Him.
What animal is the picture?
Answer: LAMB
Start Puzzle 5 from the 1st letter.
5. ↓
 Genesis 14:18–20 + Hebrews 7:1–17
A mysterious king appeared to Abraham, bringing bread and wine and blessing him. He was also described as a priest of the Most High God. Hebrews later uses this figure extensively when explaining Christ's priesthood.
Who was he?
Answer: MELCHIZEDEK
 Start Puzzle 6 from the 1st letter.
6. →
Exodus 16:4–15 + John 6:30–35
Israel was hungry in the wilderness, and God provided food from heaven. Generations later, Jesus referred to this provision while explaining that there was a greater bread from heaven.
What was the wilderness food called?
Answer: MANNA
Start Puzzle 7 from the 1st letter.
7. ↓
 Genesis 6:13–22 + 1 Peter 3:18–21
One family was preserved while judgment came upon the world around them. Peter later refers to this event while discussing salvation and draws a connection between the water of that event and Christian baptism.
What was the means of preservation?
Answer: ARK

PASSOVER ↓
↳ S → SERPENT →
↳ T ↓ TABERNACLE

↳ M → MANNA

One small design point: Puzzle 2's “3rd letter” is P, so that would not connect to TABERNACLE. The chain above needs the connection point to be T, which is the 5th letter of SERPENT. So when we turn this into the final graphic, I'll use the 5th letter, not 3rd.
That kind of checking is exactly what we need to do before designing the graphic.
